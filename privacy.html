First Light Bible — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

First Light Bible ("First Light", "the app", "we") is an offline Bible app published by the First Light Bible team, India. It needs no account and no sign-in, and it does not collect personal information in order to work. This policy explains what stays on your phone, what leaves it, and why.

1. What stays on your phone

Your highlights, bookmarks, notes, reading progress, reading-plan check-offs, quiz streak, Verse of the Day bookmarks, AI chat history, reminder time, chosen tradition and translation, display name and all other settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

You can export them as a backup file (You → Backup & export), clear the AI chat history (You → Clear chat history), or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

2. AI Bible Chat and "Explain this verse"

When you send a question in Ask First Light, or tap "Explain this verse", the app sends the text of your question, the recent messages of that conversation and, for explanations, the verse text to our server (hosted on Vercel). Our server forwards it to Google's Gemini API to generate the answer and returns the answer to the app.

  • The request carries no name, email, phone number or advertising identifier. Like any internet request it includes your IP address, which Vercel and Google may record in standard server logs for security and abuse prevention.
  • We do not store your questions or answers on our server. Google processes API requests under Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Chat history is kept only on your phone and can be cleared at any time.
  • If you prefer not to send anything to the AI service, simply do not use Ask First Light; every other feature works fully offline.

3. Purchases (First Light Pro)

First Light Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or bank details. To confirm your subscription on this device, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-user ID generated on your phone and the purchase receipt from Google Play. RevenueCat's privacy policy applies to that processing. You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Subscriptions.

4. Notifications

The daily prayer reminder and feast-day reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data leaves your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

5. Sharing

When you share a verse as text or as an image card, the app hands the content to the app you choose (WhatsApp, Instagram, email and so on). What happens next is governed by that app's policy.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: only for AI Bible Chat, subscription checks and opening links.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: only for the reminders you turn on.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files beyond the image you choose to share or the backup file you choose to save or restore.

7. No ads, no tracking, no analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs. We do not sell or rent any data, because we hold none.

8. Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

9. Your rights and data deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you are always in control: export, clear or delete it from the app or by uninstalling. If you believe we hold any information about you (for example an email you sent us), write to the address below and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date, and inside the app under You → Privacy Policy.
